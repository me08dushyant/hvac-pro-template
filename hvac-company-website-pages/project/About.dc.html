02 / ORIGIN

Founded on an unglamorous frustration.

In 2018 our founders kept being called in to rescue finished cleanrooms that could not be qualified. The panels were fine. The air handler was fine. What was missing was anyone who had thought about the two together — and anyone willing to sign for the result.

So we built the firm we kept wishing existed: designers who attend commissioning, validators on staff rather than hired at the end, and a service team that inherits the drawings instead of guessing at them.

03 / HOW WE WORK

Three commitments that decide everything else.

01

The report is the product

A room nobody can evidence is not finished, however good it looks. We are not done until the dossier is.

02

No blame surface

Design, build, validate, maintain — same signature on all four. There is nowhere for a problem to hide between contracts.

03

Chosen for year five

We specify equipment we can still repair when the warranty is long gone and the supplier has changed distributor.

04 / TIMELINE

Eight years, four decisions that mattered.

2018 Four people, one office Started as an HVAC design consultancy in Uttara serving local pharmaceutical manufacturers.
2020 First turnkey handover A full ISO 7 oral solid dosage suite: air handlers, panels, and qualification documentation on one contract.
2022 Validation brought in-house Bought our own calibrated instrumentation and hired a permanent DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ team. No more third-party scheduling.
2025 Workshop and spares depot Opened in Gazipur for panel fabrication, air handler assembly, and same-week spare availability.
